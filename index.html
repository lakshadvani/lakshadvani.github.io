I make LLM agents reliable enough to trust in production.

My research starts from a simple question: when an agent says a task is done, how do we know it actually is? I study how agents fail silently, how to verify their reasoning rather than only their answers, and how much of an agent's plan can be read from its hidden states before it acts.

I turn that work into systems that run at scale: post-training pipelines for large language models, agentic systems for IT service management, and inference optimization for serving at scale. Before ServiceNow, I adapted open-weight models for enterprise NLP at Qualtrics.
